## How to test

Speak each phrase via Shift+Alt+S (or Ctrl+Alt+V), capture the final transcript
text. Hot-term targets are **bold**. Score each term:

- ✓ exact (spelled correctly, including case — `iPhone` not `iphone`)
- ~ close (right concept, wrong form — `iPhone` → `爱疯`)
- ✗ miss (garbled or different concept)

## Scoring & decision rule

After running both flag=1 and flag=0 columns:

- **biasing 有效**: ≥3 个 hot term 从 `~`/`✗` 改进到 `✓` 且无新增 `✗` → **keep flag=1**.
- **biasing 抢词** (over-bias regression): 出现新的 `✗`,而 flag=0 同 phrase 该 term 是 `✓` 的 case ≥1 → **flag=0**,下次调 `TRIE_LAMBDA` 或重选 trie terms。
- **无明显差异**: <3 个改进 + 无 regression → **flag=1 留着** (零成本,可能在长尾上有 marginal 收益)。
